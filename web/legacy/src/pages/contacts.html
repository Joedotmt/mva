Visiting Luqa Barracks by car

Dear members,

As you well know and emphasised on is the fact that everyone needs to inform us a day before entering Luqa Barracks by car. For us to help you get access to our office you need to give us the licence plate number, type of car, and colour.

Thank you all for your cooperation.

Għażież membri,

Bħal ma tafu u enfasizzajna il-fatt hu li kulhadd irrid jinfurmana ġurnata qabel tiġu u tidħlu ġewwa Luqa Barracks bil-karozza tagħkhom. Sabiex aħna ngħinukhom iġġibu aċċess għal offiċju tridu ttuna in-numru, t-tip, u l-għamla tal-karozza.

Nirringrazzjakhom tal-kooperazzjoni.
