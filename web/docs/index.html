Malta Veterans Association

Become a member today Donate

The Malta Veterans Association was established to foster comradeship through regular social events and to provide a vital support network for all veterans. Our mission is to ensure that every veteran can lead a fulfilling life after their service. We offer a platform where veterans can discuss any concerns and receive the guidance they need.

CAFM is our Patron. At present Brigadier Clinton J. O'Neill
