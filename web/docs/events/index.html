7 June 2026

Today we honour the courage of ordinary Maltese who stood up for dignity and justice on Sette Giugno 1919. As veterans, we recognise the same values that bind service: duty, sacrifice, and solidarity. On this national day we remember the four who fell, Carmelo Abela, Manuel Attard, Guze Bajada and Wenzu Dyer, the families they left behind, and the generations who inherited their resolve.

27 February 2026

The Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Malta Veterans Association was successfully held on Friday, 27th February 2026, at the WO & SGTs Mess Barracks, Luqa.

The meeting was well attended and provided members with the opportunity to discuss matters concerning the Association, its activities, governance, and future direction.

In accordance with the Association’s statute, the current committee was formally dissolved and the election of the committee took place.

Following the voting process, the members voted in favour of retaining the same committee for the coming term. Tony Grech was re-elected as President, while Maria Gatt was re-elected as Vice-President.

The Malta Veterans Association would like to thank all members who attended and participated in the AGM, contributing towards its successful conclusion. The continued support and involvement of our members remain essential to the strength and future of the Association.

25 April, 2025

Anzac Day Commemoration at Pietà Military Cemetery

Each year, Australia and New Zealand co-host a special service to commemorate Anzac Day on 25th April at Pietà Military Cemetery. This event honours the Australians and New Zealanders who lost their lives at Gallipoli in 1915 and in subsequent military and peacekeeping operations. It also acknowledges Malta's role in caring for the sick and wounded during times of conflict.

Remembrance Day - 10 November, 2024

Also known as Poppy Day, Malta marked Remembrance Day at the eleventh hour. In most countries, Remembrance Day is observed at the eleventh hour on the eleventh day of the eleventh month.

The Armistice, an agreement to end World War I fighting as a prelude to peace negotiations, began at 11am on November 11, 1918. Also remembered the soldiers who gave their lives in the many conflicts since then.

This year the MVA, took an active part in the Remembrance Day Parade on 10th November 2024, by providing a squad made up from members of the Association, The Association also paraded its Standard for the first time on the occasion. A wreath was laid on behave of all Veterans.

4 October, 2024

President Anthony Grech, Secretary Keith Greengrass and PRO Marc Schembri had a courtesy meeting with the Leader of the Opposition Dr Bernard Grech and the Shadow Ministers Darren Carabott and Paula Mifsud Bonnici. During this meeting they stressed the importance of the soldiers being encouraged to continue to provide service even after they have passed the 25 years of service. This is apart from the fact that those who do not continue should be given the necessary help to find opportunities after they have finished the service. They also said that currently in this association there are around 380 members - all former soldiers in the army. For his part the Leader of the Nationalist Party Bernard Grech claimed the importance of the Armed Forces of our country, while he reiterated that the Nationalist Party will continue to work so that the soldiers have the best possible conditions.
