As in customary on these Feast days, this year too, the Armed Forces of Malta will celebrate the solemn function of the beloved Christmas night on 24th December.   The Church will be open in the evening from 6:45pm onwards.  While the Veterans Association urges members to attend, we ask that everyone be in their seats by 7:20pm.  The Liturgical Service starts at 7:30pm with Christmas Carols. 
After the celebration of the Eucharist, we will give each other good wishes, while we taste together some mince pies and mulled wine.   
When arriving in Luqa, the best access to the Barracks and the Church is from the roundabout and from the Old Airport Car Park.
