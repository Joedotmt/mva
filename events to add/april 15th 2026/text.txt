Dear members.
As planned, today, April 15, 2026, members of the Committee had a Courtesy Meeting with the Prime Minister at his Office in Berga-ta-Castille. The following points were discussed at the meeting:-
a. Service and Social Services Pensions;
b. Collective Agreements made vis-à-vis us Veterans;
c. Service and Veterans’ Widows’ Pensions;
d. The Pink Card;
e. Compensation for Our Work Over the Years;
f. EVRF and Their Engagement (at the request of Veteran members who are EVRFs);
g. The Situation in Cases of Injustices Affecting Veterans.
This post is being uploaded for the information of members. As you can see, we are doing our best to discuss the needs of our veterans. I would like to appeal to those who are not members, but followers of this page, to become members because the more support we have, the more we can speak face to face.
