Dear members.
4 members of the Committee, Tony Grech, Noel Mifsud, Aristide Galea and Roger Muscat, had a Courtesy Meeting with a Delegation of the Nationalist Party led by Dr Alex Borg, Leader of the Opposition. At the meeting, which was a cordial one, we raised the following points:-
a. Service and Social Services Pensions;
b. Collective Agreements made vis-à-vis us Veterans;
c. Service and Veterans' Widows' Pensions;
d. The Pink Card;
e. Compensation for our work over the years;
f. EVRF and their Engagement (at the request of Veteran members who are EVRFs)
This post is being uploaded so that members are informed. Something that definitely struck us was asking how many Veterans we think are left and how many are members. On behalf of the Committee I appeal to anyone who is not a member, but a follower of this page, to become a member because as they say where there is a Community there is strength. Thank you Greetings
