Past Events

29 September 2023

The latest charity gala for the MALTESE VETERAN REAL ESTATE ASSOCIATION was hosted on September 29th at the Grand Marina. Our board members gathered to discuss future initiatives that will help veterans and their families integrate better into civilian life. We are grateful to everyone who attended and showed support. Your contributions have enabled us to provide vital services to those who have served our nation.

11 August 2023

Our commemorative event held at the Naval Base was a resounding success! Representatives from various branches attended the ceremony at the memorial grounds, honoring the USS Liberty victims. Families and veterans came together to pay their respects and share meaningful moments of remembrance.

The ceremony included a formal flag presentation, followed by a memorial service. This event not only commemorates those we've lost but also strengthens the bonds within our veteran community. Special thanks to all who participated in making this a memorable occasion.

July 21, 2023

We had another incredible social gathering where members and their families came together for an afternoon of camaraderie and relaxation at our community center.

22 April, 2023

Memorial Dedication Ceremony and Garden Gathering took place at our headquarters. Veterans and their families gathered for this memorable occasion where new memorial plaques were unveiled. The event included refreshments, speeches from distinguished guests, and a moment of silence for our fallen comrades. The beautiful garden setting provided the perfect backdrop for reflecting on the sacrifices made by those who served our nation.

3 March, 2023

Meeting and Lunch Reception for new members held at our Garden Pavilion.

New members were formally welcomed into the Veterans Association with a special reception. The event featured opening remarks from our leadership, an introduction of new members to the community, and opportunities for networking. Members enjoyed a catered lunch while discussing upcoming initiatives and ways to support our veteran community.

Welcome to all the new members, and we look forward to your active participation in our mission to serve those who served.

13 February, 2023

REMEMBRANCE AND MEMORIAL SERVICE

Annual Memorial Service for fallen comrades. Memorial flowers and wreaths laid at the National Veterans Memorial.

Guest Speakers addressed the gathering with powerful tributes, followed by a wreath-laying ceremony and moment of silence. Veterans from all branches attended to honor those who made the ultimate sacrifice.

We appreciate everyone who participated in this solemn and meaningful ceremony.
