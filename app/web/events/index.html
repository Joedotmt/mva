12 August 2025

The MVA proudly took part in the 83rd Anniversary of Operation Pedestal Commemorative Event, honoring the crucial British convoy known locally as Il-Konvoj ta’ Santa Marija which provided desperately needed supplies to Malta during the siege of World War II in 1942.

The ceremony was held at Pinto Wharf, Valletta, on Tuesday, 12 August 2025. The MVA was represented by Ms. M. Gatt, Vice President and wreath layer, together with Mr. N. Gafa, who was the Standard Bearer
